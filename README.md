# ios-camera-app-starter

A SwiftUI camera app starter for iOS 18, with tap-to-lock subject tracking.

> **Status:** early. The camera is being moved over from an existing app and is
> not usable yet.

## Planned for v0.1

- Photo capture in 4:3, 16:9 and 1:1
- Video recording
- Live Photos
- Portrait mode
- Automatic macro switching on supported iPhones
- Zoom by preset, pinch, and a front-camera slider
- Flash, self-timer, tap to focus
- Tap a subject to lock focus on it and keep tracking it as it moves
  (animals, people, faces, or whatever stands out in the frame)
- Tilt guidance for level shots
- Location tagging

## Requirements

- iOS 18.0 or later, iPhone only
- Xcode 26
- A physical device. The simulator has no camera.
